# SUCCESS CRITERIA

To ensure the game is successful and meets stakeholder expectations, several clear criteria must be met.
First, the game should allow omnidirectional movement, meaning the player can move left, right, and
jump using standard keys (W, A, S, D or arrow keys). This basic control scheme is essential for
platforming and gives players the freedom to navigate. The movement should feel smooth and
responsive, allowing players to dodge hazards and interact with the world properly.

A main menu must be implemented that lets players start the game and view their score or instructions.
This menu should be easy to navigate and visually consistent with the game’s retro style. It should also
include options for restarting after death or returning to the home screen. The game over screen is
equally important it needs to appear as soon as the player loses all health, with a clear option to restart or
return to the main menu.

The game must include enemies that spawn in waves rather than all at once. These enemies will become
progressively tougher with each level. This ties in with the enemy buff feature, where enemies increase in
speed and health every round, making the game harder as the player progresses. Enemies should
interact with the player by dealing damage upon contact and reducing the player&#39;s health bar, which will
be shown in the top right corner. To give players a fighting chance, a short invincibility window (around 3
seconds) will be activated after taking damage to prevent instant deaths.

Players must be able to shoot enemies using a gun, triggered by a specific key (like the space bar or a
mouse click). However, instead of infinite ammo, players will start with 30 rounds and must collect more
from around the map. This introduces a tactical element to the game where conserving bullets becomes
essential. Ammo collection encourages exploration and adds tension in combat.

A score system should be active throughout gameplay. Players should earn points for defeating enemies,
completing levels, and possibly picking up score multipliers through powerups. These powerups will
appear every 10 seconds, last for 15, and include boosts such as invulnerability, speed, and jump height.
This adds variety and keeps gameplay engaging.

The game will also feature a victory screen, which is triggered after the player completes the final boss
level. This screen should clearly display that the game is complete and give options to return to the menu
or restart the game. The boss level itself will be more difficult than regular levels, with a boss that spawns
on the top platform and follows a set pattern of attacks. Defeating this boss will be the final challenge and
a key part of the game&#39;s structure.

Gameplay should be spread across multiple levels that get progressively harder. These levels will include
a range of platforms, which players must climb using ladders or ropes. Players must be able to interact
with these objects using “W” or up arrows to climb and down to descend. Some platforms may be locked
behind puzzles or hazards. Gravity will always be active, meaning players can fall off platforms and need
to time their jumps precisely.

To add depth, varied enemy types will be introduced. Some may use different weapons or appear in
different sizes, making each encounter feel fresh. Players will also have different weapons to choose
from, such as guns, swords, or bows, giving them the chance to adapt their playstyle. At the start of the
game, a character selection screen should allow players to choose from multiple playable characters,
each potentially with slight visual or gameplay differences.

Finally, there should be clear victory conditions for example, defeating the boss on the final level or
reaching a particular score threshold. If these conditions are met and the game successfully incorporates
the above systems in a smooth, enjoyable, and balanced way, then I’ll be confident that my project has
achieved its goals. All these features, together with solid testing and user feedback, form the full success
criteria that I’ll use to measure the effectiveness and completeness of my game.
