# PROBLEM DESCRIPTION
I intend to make a retro platform game similar to the original Donkey Kong game. Users will be tasked
with getting their playable character to the top platform (out of 5) of the game whilst avoiding obstacles
which will be thrown at them by the ‘boss’/’villain’ of the game. Once the boss is defeated, they will be
able to rescue the boss’s hostage. Once this is done the player will receive a medal and well done. They
will then be given the option to play again or go to the title screen. If the player is hit by an obstacle, they
will be knocked off their platform causing them to lose the game. A game over screen would be displayed
and they will be given the option to either play again or go to the title screen. Players will use their
keyboard to interact with their character (left and right arrow keys for forwards and back, space bar to
jump, up arrow to climb up a ladder). Players will be able to pick up weapons and/or powerups across the
platforms to help them defeat any enemies and destroy obstacles. The score and a timer will be displayed
at the top of the screen. This game will only be playable for one player at a time however, players will be
able to compare scores and times on completion of the game.
